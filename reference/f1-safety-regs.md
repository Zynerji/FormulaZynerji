# FIA Formula 1 Safety Regulations — Reference Digest

## 1. Car safety structures
- **Survival cell / monocoque:** carbon-epoxy + aluminium honeycomb + Kevlar penetration layer; intact after all crash tests. Carbon monocoques since 1981; mandatory crash testing since 1985; all tests before pre-season testing since 2012. Frontal dynamic test 15 m/s (~900–925 kg incl. dummy); connection must hold 120% of test forces elastically.
- **Halo (FIA 8869-2018):** grade-5 titanium, ~7–9 kg; static load **125 kN** (~12.7 t) ≥5 s, no failure, extraction preserved. ~17% survival-rate increase. Saved Leclerc (2018), Grosjean (2020), Hamilton (2021), Zhou (2022). Spec part, FIA-approved supplier.
- **Front/side/rear crash structures:** deformable, bolted to cell; positions specified, internal construction free. Front tested twice (second on damaged structure). Side: anti-intrusion Kevlar/Zylon panels. Rear: at/behind gearbox.
- **Roll structures (primary + secondary):** combined vertical/lateral/longitudinal (~105/60/70 kN); deflection ≤25 mm; failure confined to top 100 mm.
- **Collapsible steering column.** **Headrest** (since 1996). **Wheel tethers:** Zylon, three per wheel (single 1999, second 2011, third 2018) — after Surtees 2009. **Extractable seat** (FIA 8862-2009): static 20 g fwd/15 g rear/10 g lat + 20 g dynamic sled.

## 2. Driver protection & equipment
- **HANS/FHR** (mandatory 2003) — prevents basilar skull fracture; zero such fatalities since.
- **Helmet FIA 8860-2018 ABP** — carbon shell, Nomex liner, ballistic protection tested vs projectile at 250 km/h; visor opening lowered 10 mm.
- **Fire-resistant clothing FIA 8856-2018** (suit/underwear/balaclava/gloves/socks/shoes; 10-yr life). HTI: suit ~18 s, gloves 9.5–13 s, shoes 11 s, underwear 5 s.
- **Harness FIA 8853-2016** (6-point, HANS-compatible, Ti camlock, 5-yr life; dynamic test raised to 67 km/h / 60+ g).
- **Biometric gloves** (2018) — HR/SpO₂ to ADR2/medical. **ADR** (since 1997) — 3-axis high-g (≥250 g)+low-g, speed/throttle/steering/yaw. **High-speed cockpit camera** (2016, up to 400 fps). **Medical Warning Light** — auto at ≥18 g, upward-facing.

## 3. Homologation standards (categories)
Fuel cells FT5/FT3.5/FT3-1999 (TL01; FT5 = 2000 lb tear, 5-yr +1 recert); helmet 8860-2018 ABP / 8859-2015; clothing 8856-2018; harness 8853-2016; seat 8862-2009; FHR guidelines; halo 8869-2018; fire extinguisher TL06 (AFFF); ADR2; wheel tethers (restraint-cable guidelines); circuit barriers FIA 3501-2017 (TecPro). Items carry hologram + expiry; expired = not permitted.

## 4. Circuit & operational safety
- **Grade 1** for F1: ≥3.5 km, ≥12 m wide, 30–100 m run-off, medical centre (≥2 resus doctors + 2 surgeons), helicopter evac, pit-lane separation.
- **Barriers:** TecPro (FIA 3501-2017, mandatory new Grade 1), SAFER, tyre walls (legacy), Armco (low-risk). Shift from gravel to asphalt run-off (controlled deceleration, fewer rollovers).
- **Flags** as safety signals + electronic marshalling panels.
- **SC** (field bunch) / **VSC** (delta time, introduced 2015 after Bianchi 2014). **Medical car** (Aston Martin DBX707) + FIA medical delegate, auto-deploy on ADR trigger. **Extraction team** trained to halo/HANS geometry. **Scrutineering:** no-waiver safety checks; failure = no start.

## 5. Philosophy
1994 Imola (Ratzenberger + Senna) was the watershed — 27 corners modified within months; headrests 1996; no WC fatality 1994→2014. 2014 Bianchi → VSC (2015) + accelerated halo (2018, mandated over resistance: "if it saves one life it is justified" — saved ≥4 within 4 years). FIA pattern: incident → countermeasure → mandatory standard → progressively tightened → no opt-out. **Car-design freedom and rigid safety standards are orthogonal.**

## 6. Minimum non-negotiable safety floor for an open class (prioritised)
1. Survival cell crash-test regime (frontal ≥15 m/s; roll 105/60/70 kN, ≤25 mm; side intrusion; rear) — pass pre-season, witnessed.
2. Halo / FIA 8869-equivalent (125 kN), no removal for aero.
3. Homologated fuel cell (FT5-equivalent).
4. ≥2 wheel tethers/wheel (Zylon-equivalent).
5. Anti-intrusion side panels.
6. Helmet 8860-2018 ABP; HANS; 8856-2018 clothing; 8853-2016 harness; 8862-2009 extractable seat.
7. ADR (≥250 g), Medical Warning Light (≥18 g), cockpit camera (≥200 fps).
8. Fire suppression (TL06) + collapsible steering column.
9. Grade-2-min circuit; run-off + TecPro/SAFER; medical car + trauma physician + helicopter; extraction team; VSC/marshalling within 30 s; no-waiver scrutineering.

**Key principle:** you can permit unrestricted aero, exotic materials, novel powertrains and adjustable everything *while* mandating exact crash-test pass criteria and equipment standards that are fully non-negotiable. The 2018 halo is the proof.
