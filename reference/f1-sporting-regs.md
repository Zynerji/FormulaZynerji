# FIA Formula 1 Sporting Regulations — Reference Digest

## 1. Document structure
2025 = single numbered-article document (~107–119 pp). 2026 reorganised into Section B (B1 Organisation, B2 Format, B3–B11 Scrutineering/Tyres/Personnel/Media/Testing). Substantive rules largely unchanged from 2025 except DRS renamed to "active aerodynamic system".

2025 article headings (functional building blocks): Regulations; General Undertaking; General Conditions; Licences; Championship; World Championship; Dead Heat; Competitor Applications; Livery & Numbers; Track Running; Promoter; Organisation; Insurance; FIA Delegates; Officials; Communications; Protests/Appeals; Sanctions; Press/Media; Meetings; Covering Components; DRS; Personnel; Team Shutdown; PU Shutdown; General Safety; Spare Cars; PU Usage; Restricted-Number Components; Tyres; Driver Changes; Driving; Pit Lane; Weighing; Refuelling; Practice; Free Practice; Qualifying & Sprint Qualifying; Parc Fermé (pre); Grid; Sprint Start; Race Start; Formation/Aborted/False Start; Safety Car; VSC; Suspending/Resuming; Finish; Parc Fermé (post); Points/Awards.

## 2. Race weekend format
**Standard (3 days):** FP1/FP2 (Fri 60 min ea), FP3 (Sat 60), Qualifying (Sat, Q1/Q2/Q3 = 45 min total), Race (Sun, 305 km / 2 hr max).
**Sprint weekend:** FP1 + GP Qualifying (Fri); Sprint Qualifying + Sprint (~100 km/60 min) (Sat); GP (Sun). 6 sprint rounds in 2026.

**Qualifying knockout:** Q1 (18 min, slowest 5–6 out), Q2 (15 min, slowest 5–6 out), Q3 (12–13 min, top-10 pole shoot-out). Sprint Qualifying SQ1/SQ2/SQ3 (12/10/8 min).

**Race:** min 305 km (Monaco 260); hard 2-hr clock; 3-hr absolute window incl. stoppages. Mandatory ≥2 different dry compounds in a dry race (≈1 forced stop). Pit lane 80 km/h.

**Shortened-race points scales:** <25% → top-5 (6-4-3-2-1); 25–49% → top-9 (13…); 50–74% → top-10 (19…); ≥75% → full.

## 3. Points & championship
Race top-10: **25-18-15-12-10-8-6-4-2-1**. Sprint top-8: **8-7-6-5-4-3-2-1**. Fastest-lap bonus **abolished for 2025**. Both drivers' points count for Constructors'. Tie-break: most wins, then most 2nds, 3rds, … (quali/FL irrelevant).

## 4. Penalties & stewarding
4 stewards incl. a driver steward. Ladder: reprimand (4/season → 5th = 10-place grid) → 5 s → 10 s time penalty → drive-through → 10 s stop-go → grid penalty → DSQ → race ban. **Superlicence penalty points:** 12 in rolling 12 months = 1-race ban; expire after 12 months.

**Flags:** green, single/double yellow, red, blue (3 ignored = penalty), white, chequered, black, black-and-orange (meatball), black-and-white (conduct warning), yellow-red surface.

**Safety Car:** field bunches behind SC, no overtaking, pit lane initially closed; lapped cars may un-lap; restart at SC line. **VSC:** ECU delta time (~30% slower) per marshalling sector, preserves gaps, ends 10–15 s after "VSC ENDING". **Track limits:** four wheels beyond white line; three-strikes → black-and-white → penalty/lap deletion.

## 5. Entry & eligibility
Teams apply + entry fee (new-constructor anti-dilution fee ~$200M). Max 26 cars/13 teams; 22/11 from 2026 (Cadillac). Exactly 2 cars/team. Must build primary structure; customer cars limited to listed components.

**Superlicence:** age 18; Grade A licence; FIA theory test; 300 km test in representative car; ≥80% of two seasons in eligible single-seaters; **40 superlicence points in best 3-year window** (F2/IndyCar = 40 for win; F3/FE/WEC Hypercar/Super Formula = 30; etc.). Cost ≈ €11,453 + ~€2,313/point.

**Testing:** restricted; 3-day pre-season; young-driver test; **ATR sliding scale** (320 WT runs + 2,000 CFD baseline; 70% champion → 115% last; reset twice/yr) — primary parity lever.

## 6. Open-class sporting considerations
F1 uses **no BoP** (Tombazis: never artificially boost/slow a car). Convergence instead via: **ATR sliding scale**, **cost cap**, **token systems**. Parc-fermé scope (broad = rewards quali setup, curbs cost; narrow = more strategy, more cost). Tyre/pit rules (two-compound + mandatory stop) manufacture overtaking even when pace diverges. Qualifying knockout stops a slow car taking pole. Alternatives debated/rejected in F1: reverse-grid sprint, success ballast (DTM/WTCR). SC/VSC as informal equalisers (controversial). Superlicence = meritocratic entry filter + fan-followable ladder.
